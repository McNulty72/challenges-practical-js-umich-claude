Solution for Challenge n1-03: Savings Plan

Challenge description

Compute the months needed to achieve at least a savings amount goal of 10,000 with a end of month deposit of 300 and monthly interest rate of 0.25%.

Logs the months needed and the final savings amount to the console. Additionally logs to the console the savings amount at end of each of the first twelve months

JavaScript concepts included in this challenge

  • While loops
  • For loops
  • Exit conditions
